1. Introduction & Overview
Modernizing Ethiopian freight transport through digital disintermediation.

1.1 System Executive Summary

HabeshaFreight is an enterprise-grade digital freight marketplace engineered specifically to address structural inefficiencies within the Ethiopian logistics sector. By serving as an integrated ecosystem, the platform digitally connects Shippers requiring cargo transport with verified Independent Drivers and Fleet Operators. Through transparency, real-time tracking, financial escrow, and structured bidding, HabeshaFreight standardizes freight transactions across key economic corridors.

2. Problem Statement
2. Statement of Problem
Structural bottlenecks severely hindering the efficiency of Ethiopian road freight.

01 — Informal Broker Dependency

Cost / Delay

Traditional freight logistics in Ethiopia relies on non-standardized informal brokers (Delalas) operating without centralized oversight.

  • Opaque broker commissions (10%-25% markups)
  • Informal verbal agreements leading to frequent disputes
  • Lack of accountability for lost or damaged goods

02 — High Backhaul Inefficiency

Resource Waste

Trucks frequently return completely empty after completing a primary delivery run from Addis Ababa to regional cities.

  • Up to 40% of total travel miles run without paid cargo
  • Wasted fuel and excessive vehicle wear-and-tear
  • Increased baseline freight prices to offset empty return trips

03 — Trust & Payment Risks

Financial Danger

Predominantly cash-based payments create major financial friction and security liabilities for shippers and drivers alike.

  • Uncertainty regarding payment settlement upon delivery
  • Risk of carrying large sums of physical cash along transit corridors
  • Absence of formal verification or escrow protection

04 — Zero Shipment Visibility

Operational Blindness

Shippers experience zero real-time visibility once cargo is loaded onto a vehicle until it reaches its final destination.

  • Inability to track exact truck coordinates or status
  • Unpredictable delivery delays and missed schedules
  • Reliance on intermittent direct phone calls with drivers

Impact Summary on the Economy

These combined structural flaws elevate transport costs in Ethiopia to over 22% of total goods costs—nearly double the global average—directly impacting national commerce and regional trade competitiveness.

6. Actors & System Boundary

  • Cloud Storage (AWS S3 / Cloudinary): Secure document storage for driver licenses & truck photos.

7. Scope & Boundaries
7. Limitations & Scope Boundaries
Explicit constraints governing Phase 1 initial deployment and future roadmap expansion.

Phase 1 Current Limitations

  • Domestic Scope Only: Restricted exclusively to core Ethiopian primary transportation corridors.
  • Single Currency: Financial operations processed strictly in Ethiopian Birr (ETB).
  • Manual Telemetry: No dedicated IoT GPS hardware installed on trucks; milestone location updates rely on driver mobile interactions.
  • Rule-Based Matching: Static location and route sorting rather than AI-driven predictive pricing algorithms.
  • Connectivity Dependency: Intermittent cellular connectivity in rural regions may delay real-time updates.

Future Roadmap Expansion

  • Djibouti Trade Corridor: Expansion into cross-border customs integration and multi-currency clearing (USD/ETB).
  • Hardwired IoT Telematics: Direct integration with vehicle OBD-II ports for continuous GPS and speed tracking.
  • AI Dynamic Pricing Engine: Machine learning algorithms suggesting real-time optimal freight rates based on seasonal demand.
  • Localization Expansion: Native UI support for Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and Tigrinya languages.

Technical Feasibility Justification

By bounding Phase 1 scope to domestic corridors and mobile-first OTP validation, HabeshaFreight minimizes hardware deployment capital while solving the primary pain points of driver verification and payment security.

9. Functional Requirements (FR-11 to FR-20)
Requirements governing escrow locking, OTP delivery verification, and disputes.

FR-11 Bid Acceptance System shall allow Shippers to review carrier bids and accept a single winning carrier, locking the transaction state.

FR-20 Audit Logging System shall write an immutable log entry for all financial transactions, status shifts, and admin override actions.

The OTP Handshake Protocol

Two-factor cryptographic verification eliminates pickup and delivery fraud. Drivers cannot claim cargo loaded or delivered without physical possession of the recipient's secure OTP.

Dispute Resolution Pathway

Triggering a dispute freezes the escrow account. Administrators review uploaded bill of lading documents and messaging logs before executing a partial or complete refund.

11. Business Rules
11. Key Business Rules (BR-01 to BR-05)
Strict domain invariants enforced programmatically by the core system engine.

BR-01: Vehicle Capacity Enforcement

Safety Invariant

A driver cannot submit a bid on a freight listing whose specified weight exceeds the verified maximum payload capacity of the assigned vehicle.

BR-02: Escrow Mandatory Before Transit

Financial Invariant

The system shall refuse to issue a Pickup OTP or allow driver dispatch until 100% of the agreed freight price is locked in the digital escrow service.

BR-03: OTP Handshake Requirement

Security Invariant

Escrow funds cannot be released to the driver's payout account under normal operational flows without successful entry of the recipient's Delivery OTP.

BR-04: Single Active Assignment

Operational Invariant

A vehicle or driver can only be assigned to one active shipment state (IN_TRANSIT) at any given time to ensure accurate availability tracking.

BR-05: Review Qualification

Trust Invariant

Users may only submit ratings and reviews for transactions that have formally reached the "COMPLETED" state.

  • API response times < 200ms for 95% of requests.
  • Geospatial load matching queries < 500ms using PostGIS spatial indexing.

Security & Encryption

  • HTTPS/TLS 1.3 enforced for all network communications.
  • Passwords hashed using Argon2id algorithm.
  • JWT access tokens with short lifespan (15 minutes) + secure refresh cookies.

Availability & Reliability

  • Target system availability of 99.9% uptime.
  • Automated daily database backups with point-in-time recovery.
  • Graceful offline mobile queueing during regional cellular dead zones.

Bandwidth & Digital Literacy

  • Mobile App payload < 15MB total bundle size.
  • High-contrast light UI optimized for direct sunlight viewing.
  • Icon-driven UI navigation supporting low digital literacy users.
